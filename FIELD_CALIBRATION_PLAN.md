# RS 18,883 Field Calibration Plan

## Objective

Replace the single approximate project-site visibility anchor with measured relationships among:

- 05EA002 stage and discharge;
- project water-surface elevation at HEC-RAS RS 18,883;
- rising, approximately flat and falling hydrograph limbs;
- exposure of the main floodplain near El. 650.20 m CGVD28;
- ponding at the former El. 649.60 m low pocket;
- actual access and working-platform bearing condition.

## Highest-value installation

Install a temporary vented pressure transducer or barometrically compensated non-vented logger at RS 18,883. Tie the logger reference elevation to CGVD28 by survey. Record at 15-minute intervals.

Minimum metadata:

- logger make, model and serial number;
- sensor reference elevation and surveyed benchmark;
- installation and removal times in UTC;
- barometric compensation method;
- pre-installation and post-removal manual depth checks;
- estimated measurement uncertainty;
- photographs showing the sensor, river edge, main floodplain and low pocket.

## Manual observation alternative

When a logger is unavailable, obtain surveyed or staff-gauge WSE observations at least daily while the project WSE is between approximately 650.50 and 650.05 m, and more frequently near 650.20 m.

For every observation, record:

- UTC time;
- project WSE in CGVD28;
- measurement method and uncertainty;
- whether the main floodplain is visible and drained;
- whether the low pocket is ponded;
- qualitative access bearing: unsuitable, marginal or suitable;
- photo reference and observer notes.

Enter observations in `project_site_observations.csv`.

## Sampling priorities

1. Falling-limb observations near the operational threshold.
2. A rising-limb passage through the same discharge range.
3. At least one approximately flat period.
4. Observations after similar 05EA002 discharge but different antecedent rainfall/storage conditions.
5. Confirmation of the as-built elevation and drainage behavior of the former 649.60 m pocket.

## Minimum evidence for model changes

Do not replace the current RS 18,883 transfer from one isolated measurement. Promotion requires:

- at least six surveyed project-WSE observations;
- at least three rising and three falling observations where practical;
- paired 05EA002 observations within 30 minutes;
- coverage below, near and above the 650.20 m threshold;
- no unresolved datum discrepancy;
- residual and leave-one-observation-out checks;
- separate rising/falling curves only when their separation is repeatable and materially larger than measurement uncertainty;
- retention of the prior transfer for rollback.

## Construction release

No statistical or hydraulic fit replaces the field release decision. Work release remains conditional on:

- project WSE at or below the applicable threshold;
- main floodplain visibly drained;
- no credible renewed rise;
- acceptable access and working-platform bearing;
- manageable rutting, pumping and dewatering;
- confirmation that the low pocket no longer independently controls readiness.
